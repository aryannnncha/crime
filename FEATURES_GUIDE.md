# 🛡️ Crime Map - Complete Features Guide

## Welcome to Women's Safety Crime Intelligence Platform
A comprehensive safety tool for women traveling in Uttar Pradesh with real-time crime data and smart route recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Quick Start Features

### 1. 🔍 **Search Areas**
- **Location**: Search bar at the top of screen
- **How to Use**: Type zone name (e.g., "Gomti Nagar")
- **Results**: Shows crime count and risk level
- **Action**: Click result to zoom to that area on map

### 2. 🌙 **Dark Mode**
- **Location**: Moon icon (🌙) in header
- **Effect**: Switches app to dark theme (easier on eyes at night)
- **Persistence**: Your preference is saved automatically
- **Tip**: Great for night navigation safety

### 3. 📊 **Crime Statistics**
- **Location**: Chart icon (📊) in header
- **Shows**: 
  - Total crimes in current city
  - Top 3 crime types with percentages
  - 3 safest areas
  - 3 most risky areas
- **Use for**: Understanding local safety landscape
- **Click to Close**: Toggle on/off

### 4. ❓ **Help & FAQ**
- **Location**: Question mark (❓) in header
- **Covers**: All major features and how to use them
- **Quick Reference**: Built-in tutorial for each feature
- **First Visit**: Auto-shows 5-slide onboarding tour

### 5. ⚙️ **Settings**
- **Location**: Gear icon (⚙️) in header
- **Includes**:
  - Theme toggle (dark/light)
  - Emergency contacts manager
  - Data management (clear all)

## 🛣️ Route & Navigation

### 6. 💔 **Save Favorite Routes**
- **How**: Click heart icon (🤍) on any route card
- **Saves**: Start, end, distance, duration, safety score
- **Storage**: Stored locally on your device
- **Max**: Up to 50 saved routes
- **Access**: Saved routes persist even after closing app

### 7. 📤 **Share Routes**
- **Button**: Share icon (📤) on route cards
- **Options**:
  - 💬 WhatsApp - Share with friends/family instantly
  - 📧 Email - Send route details via email
  - 📋 Copy Link - Copy shareable link for messaging
- **Includes**: Full route details, safety score, distance
- **Why**: Share your planned route for accountability

### 8. 🚨 **Live Route Intelligence**
- **Trigger**: Shows during 3D journey view
- **Features**:
  - Zone detection (warns when entering risky area)
  - Crime analysis (shows crime types nearby)
  - Safety score (updates in real-time)
  - AI warnings (gets gemini suggestions for risks)
  - Night warnings (alerts for dark/unsafe times)
- **Controls**: Start Walk, My Location buttons

### 9. 📍 **My Location Navigation**
- **Button**: "My Location" button in route panel
- **Effect**: Recalculates route from your current GPS position
- **3D View**: Enables first-person perspective journey
- **Bearing**: Rotates camera to match travel direction

## 🔴 Incident & Crime Information

### 10. 📍 **Incident Details Popup**
- **How to Open**: Click on any red crime marker on map
- **Shows**:
  - Location & address
  - Crime type & severity level
  - Time of incident (estimated hour)
  - Associated tags
  - Related incidents in same location
  - Safety tips specific to area
- **Actions**:
  - Center on Map - Zoom to exact location
  - Search Area - Shows other crimes nearby
  - Report Incident - Flag for verification

### 11. 🎨 **Map Color Coding**
- **Yellow-Green (Low Risk)**: Few crimes, generally safe
- **Orange (Moderate Risk)**: Some incidents, standard precautions
- **Red (High Risk)**: Multiple incidents, high caution needed
- **Darker Red (Critical)**: Very dangerous, use extreme caution

## 👥 Emergency Contacts & SOS

### 12. 🚨 **Emergency Contacts Manager**
- **Location**: Settings panel (⚙️) → Emergency Contacts section
- **How to Add**: 
  - Enter name (e.g., "Mom")
  - Enter phone number
  - Click "Add" button
- **Manage**: Remove contacts anytime
- **Max**: 10 emergency contacts
- **Usage**: Used for SOS and safety notifications
- **Future**: Will send automated messages during emergencies

### 13. 📱 **Women Safety Module**
- **Location**: Left sidebar → Women Safety tab
- **Features**:
  - **SOS Shake**: Shake device to trigger SOS alarm
  - **Safe Walk Timer**: Timer during journey (notification when done)
  - **Emergency Dial**: Quick access to 112, 1091, 1098

## 🎮 Using Smart Routes

### Step 1: Enter Locations
- Type start location (where you are or want to start)
- Type end location (your destination)
- Click "Find Safe Route"

### Step 2: Compare Routes
- System shows 3 different route options
- Each shows:
  - **Score**: Higher = safer (0-100)
  - **Crime Spots**: How many crimes near this route
  - **Safe Spots**: Hospitals, police stations nearby
  - **Distance & Duration**: Travel time estimates

### Step 3: Choose Best Route
- Compare scores and crime counts
- Click "Use This Route" for selection
- Route highlights on map

### Step 4: Navigate Safely
- Click "Start 3D Journey" for immersive view
- See green dot (start) and red dot (end)
- Click play button to begin navigation
- Watch for zone alerts in Intelligence Panel

### Step 5: Share Route
- Click share icon to send to friends
- Via WhatsApp: Sends link + message
- Via Email: Complete route details
- Copy Link: Paste anywhere (messengers, SMS, etc)

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### Search not showing results?
- Make sure you're searching zone names in current city
- Try alternate spelling
- Check city selection in left panel

### Route not calculating?
- Check internet connection
- Verify start/end locations are valid
- Try different locations

### Dark mode not saving?
- Check browser allows localStorage
- Disable privacy restrictions
- Theme should persist automatically

### GPS not working?
- Allow location access in browser
- Try "My Location" button again
- Use manual location search instead

### Can't add emergency contacts?
- Check storage quota on device
- Clear some data first
- Maximum 10 contacts per device
